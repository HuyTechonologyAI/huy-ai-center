# Prioritized role cards
[Machine cards](../../config/organization/role_cards.json) record all 12 roles in hiring order: ai-hr-001, chief-architect, qa, security, primary-coder, devops, data, research, education, tax-legal, content, ops. Responsibilities and independent reviewers are specified in the [competency matrix](competency_matrix.md); dependencies and activation gates are in [hiring order](hiring_order.md).
Each card assigns canonical tool IDs, proposed documentation-only read/write boundaries, an R2 autonomous ceiling and empty effective permissions. R0 read-only, R1 low-impact and R2 bounded reversible work remain subject to task authorization; R3 high-impact and R4 critical actions require human review and a separate policy. No manager or delegate may raise these ceilings. Unknown scope or invalid evidence denies allocation.
